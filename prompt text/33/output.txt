Version 33: runs never lean; they bend, or say why they cannot.

A run between two square faces no longer tilts to swallow an offset. Where the faces are out of line by more than the entries can slide away, the run bends, leaving and arriving square-on as a duct should. Where what is left after sliding is too small for a dogleg of the gentlest allowed bend to step, the entries now slide the other way, apart along their faces, until the offset is enough for one; that is what the picture you sent needed, and those lanes now leave the face square and take a pair of 11.25° bends. Where the faces cannot give that much room, a three-bend S makes the small step instead, still square at both ends.

When no bends can do it, the run says so plainly instead of leaning: how far out of line the faces are and, as the case may be, that the gentlest dogleg needs more run than there is between the chambers, with both lengths given, or that it steps more than the offset and the entries can slide no further apart. Faces within half a degree of square are treated as square, and a duct between them may skew by that much and no more; a duct in section may still fall gently between two levels, as before.

Verified in a driven browser: aligned faces straight and square, a small offset absorbed by sliding, a larger one bent at the ends' limits, an offset just short of a dogleg slid apart and bent at 11.25°, three MV lanes between 1,800 chambers 250 out of line slid apart and bent once, narrow faces taking a three-bend S, the two refusals with their reasons, faces 0.3° off square still straight and 1.5° off no longer bridged by a lean, the section still falling gently, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 32, all passing with no console errors.
