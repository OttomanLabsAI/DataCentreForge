Version 26 — named groups, icons and a wheel of choices.

The ribbon now reads like a proper toolbar. Its buttons sit in captioned groups — Create (Chamber, Obstacle), View (3D view, Fit view), Manage (Chambers, Runs, Obstacles, Specs) and Settings (Drawing, File) — each with a small line icon above its label, and the counts stay on Chambers, Runs and Obstacles. Connect faces is no longer a button or a mode.

Right-click anything on the drawing and a ring of choices opens around the cursor, Inventor-style, each with its little icon and the item's name at the hub: a chamber offers Edit, Runs, Duplicate, Fit view and Delete; an obstacle Edit, Around, Over, Under (its default method), Duplicate and Delete; a run Edit, Place or Update line, Spec and Disconnect; empty space Chamber here, Obstacle here, 3D view, Fit view and Runs. In the 3D view the same rings appear on whatever you pick, and empty space offers Plan view.

Right-click a face to connect: "Connect from" begins a connection there — the status line says so — and the other face is joined either with a plain click or by right-clicking it and choosing "Connect here"; Cancel or Escape lets go. A face that already carries runs also offers its Side settings, and every face offers its chamber's editor. Right-clicking a chamber, obstacle or run selects it as well, so the ribbon lights up accordingly.

Verified in a driven browser: the group captions and icons, the counts, the absence of the old button, the chamber ring and its Edit, Escape and a click elsewhere closing the ring, a chamber added from empty space at the click, connecting by right-click from one face to another (an existing pair selected rather than duplicated, a new pair created), completing with a plain click, Escape cancelling, the obstacle ring setting a default, the run ring opening its spec, and the rings in 3D — plus the batteries from versions 14 to 25, all passing with no console errors.
