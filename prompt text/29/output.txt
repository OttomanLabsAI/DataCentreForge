Version 29: the example becomes the AMS01 site.

The Example button now lays out the AMS01 model: the 105 manholes of the MV Chamber family at their true positions and rotations, carrying their Revit marks (MV-FA4, LV25/Z1 and the rest), their types (Glent Typical, GLT LV Typical and the two LV vaults) and their element ids, so a later export of the same model refreshes them in place. Placing it fits the whole site in view, and two undos take it away again.

The export you sent carries only the family's depth planes, z1 to z6, and none of the a and b side planes the importer reads for wall and size, so on its own the tool finds no manhole family in it. The example therefore borrows the DCBuild family's side planes, giving every chamber 1,800 by 1,800 inside with a 100 wall, and takes its depths from AMS01's own planes: lid 350 above the placement level, base 2,000 below. Twenty of the instances are flagged mirrored in Revit while their axes come out right-handed, so the importer now honours the flag as well as the handedness and swaps the A and C sides of those chambers. One mark, LV2/Z2, appears twice in the model; the second now keeps its name with a count, LV2/Z2 (2), rather than being renamed to a generated reference.

Verified in a driven browser: the 105 placed in a quarter of a second with the right marks, types, positions, rotations, depths and identities, the mirrored sides, the doubled mark, the whole site in view, the Chambers window listing all 105, a run between two neighbours resolving within the moment, a second press refreshing in place, undo and redo of the whole site, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 28 including the touch ones, all passing with no console errors.
