Version 42: a Models window, encasement modelled in, and static models the dynamic ones route around.

A Models button joins the Manage group. It lists every model — every layer — with its counts: how many manholes, how many conduit runs and how many of those are encased, how many obstacles, and whether it is static or dynamic. Each model has a tick that hides the whole of it, and four more that hide only its manholes, its conduits, its encasement or its obstacles, so a model's conduits can be studied with its chambers out of the way; hiding its conduits takes their encasement with them, and hiding the model greys the four. Select takes everything shown on that model as the selection in one press, so a whole model can be moved to another layer, encased or deleted together. Hiding is only about what you see: nothing hidden stops being a keep-out.

Every element of a static model is now a keep-out for every dynamic run — its manholes, its conduit banks and their encasement alike — whatever the drawing's own Conduits avoid other chambers and Conduits avoid runs switches say. With both switches off a dynamic run still bends around a static manhole and holds its distance from a static bank; set that model dynamic and press Update routes and the same elements stop being keep-outs, leaving only the obstacles, as the switches intend.

The encasement is modelled in. The MV export now marks every bank as encased with its 100 mm offset, so the model arrives with its boxes already on and the specs it creates carry the offset. In three dimensions the box is drawn as a solid — four faces along its length rather than four dotted edges — so an encased bank reads as the concrete it is.

Verified in a driven browser: a dynamic run bending round a static manhole and keeping clear of it with both avoidance switches off, its keep-outs counting the static model's three manholes, its obstacle and its bank as a line, and all of that falling away to just the obstacle when the model is made dynamic; the Models window listing three models in Manage with their counts and four kinds each; hiding manholes leaving the conduits and obstacle, hiding encasement leaving the conduits, hiding conduits taking the encasement with them, and unticking the model hiding all of it, greying its kinds and leaving the other model alone while still keeping its distance; Select taking five elements and only two once the manholes were hidden; the 3D view building four faces per length of box and no dotted edges; the MV example marking all 77 banks encased at 100 and placing them with their boxes on three new specs; the MV model showing its conduits with its manholes hidden; and the export carrying what each model shows and hides, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 41, all passing with no console errors. The fourteenth ribbon button wrapped the header on an iPad on its side, so the ribbon tightens from 1,220 px and again from 1,080 px, and the header keeps one row from 1,024 px up; the button-count check moved to ten window buttons.
