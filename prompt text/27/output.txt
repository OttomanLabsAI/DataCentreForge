Version 27: window selection, panning by button or by fingers, pinch zoom, an Example button, and the iPad.

Drag on empty space to select the Revit way. Left to right draws a solid window and takes only what lies wholly inside it. Right to left draws a dashed crossing and takes anything the box touches, chambers, obstacles and runs alike. Whatever is selected moves together when one of it is dragged, nudges together with the arrow keys, and goes together with Delete, runs included. A plain click still selects one thing, and a click on nothing clears.

The middle mouse button drags the view, and so does the right button. A right-click that does not move still opens its ring. The wheel zooms as before, and a touchpad pinch, which arrives as a fine-grained wheel with ctrl held, zooms at a matching pace. An Example button in the Create group places the three manholes of the DCBuild export exactly as a Revit import would, with a confirmation if a drawing is already open.

On an iPad in a browser the same tools work by hand. A tap selects, a one-finger drag on empty space draws the same window or crossing box, a one-finger drag on a chamber or obstacle moves it, and two fingers pan and pinch to zoom, in plan and in 3D, where one finger orbits. A finger held still for half a second opens the ring on whatever sits under it, whether a face, a chamber, an obstacle, a run or empty space, and so stands in for the right-click: hold a face, tap Connect here, then tap the other face, and the run is made. With several things selected the ring offers Delete N for the whole set, so nothing needs a Delete key. Windows drag by their title bars with a finger, the hint text switches to the touch gestures, the header keeps to one row at iPad widths, and the page no longer scrolls, bounces or zooms itself under a gesture.

Verified in a driven browser with a mouse and with emulated touch on iPad-sized screens: window and crossing selection by drag and by finger, the group move, deleting a set by key and by ring, middle and right drag panning, the still right-click ring, the touchpad pinch and the two-finger pinch, a finger left over from a pinch doing nothing, the held-finger ring on a chamber, on empty space and on a face through to a completed run, orbit, pan and pinch by touch in 3D, a window dragged by finger, the Example button, and the header at 1024 and 1194 px on its side and 834 px upright, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 26, all passing with no console errors. Safari itself is not available in this environment, so a minute on a real iPad is worth having.
