Version 32: orbit the Revit way, about what is selected, and the whole face in elevation.

In the 3D window the buttons now follow Revit: hold shift and drag with the middle button to orbit, drag with the middle button alone to pan, and the right button pans as before; a plain drag still orbits, and a finger still does what it did. Select a chamber, an obstacle or a conduit run, on the plan or in the window, and its centre becomes the pivot: the view does not jump when the pivot moves, and from then on orbiting turns the drawing about that thing, which stays put on screen while everything else swings round it. Fit keeps the selection as the pivot too.

The elevation now draws the whole face at true scale, from lid to base, with both labelled by their levels. Each level that carries runs has a faint line across the face with its depth written at the end, and every conduit sits at its own depth and lateral offset, arrays stacking downwards row by row, so a three by two array reads as it is laid. The dotted boundary box is the family's conduit window where the chamber came from Revit with one, and otherwise the clear area inside the edge clearance, the full height of the face rather than a band around the conduits.

Verified in a driven browser: shift with the middle button orbiting without panning, the middle button alone panning without orbiting, the right button and the plain drag as before, the pivot moving to a selected chamber and to a selected run with nothing shifting on screen, an orbit that holds the selected chamber still while the others move, the face drawn at its true proportions with lid, base and level depths labelled, the conduits at their depths and the boundary box spanning the clear area, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 31, all passing with no console errors.
