Rebuilt. Connections are now real objects with their own pipe settings.

Connections list — every run shows a status dot (green placed, grey pending, red no route), its faces, and the centreline length. Click a row to select it; the × disconnects. A selected run can also be dropped with the Disconnect button or the Delete key, and you can click a pipe on the canvas to select it.

Pipe settings apply to whichever run is selected. With nothing selected the same panel edits the defaults that new connections inherit.

Pipe radius — drawn as the actual pipe body, with the centreline over it
Bend radius — fillets every direction change; if a straight is too short the radius is reduced for that bend and the row is flagged ⚠
Min straight — shortest permitted straight, including off each chamber face
Bend angles allowed — chips for 11.25 / 15 / 22.5 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90

Place line commits the route. The router leaves the internal face along its outward normal, arrives at the far internal face along its inward normal, and only ever changes direction by an angle you've ticked. It searches straight, one, two and three bend solutions and takes the fewest bends, then the shortest. If nothing satisfies the constraints it says why — "no room, reduce the minimum straight" versus "no route with these angles" are different problems.

Worth knowing: it won't fudge a route to make it work. Connect two faces at 30° with only 22.5/45/90 ticked and you get "no route" rather than a wrong angle. Placed runs re-solve automatically when you drag a chamber, so the pipe follows.

The demo has MH01·E→MH02·W placed as a straight run, and MH02·E→MH03·W left pending so you can see both states — MH03 is skewed 45°, and that run solves as a single 45° bend.
