Done — straights are now the priority, structurally.

The scoring charges run length by direction: metres spent on the run's own axes (the directions it leaves and enters the manholes) count at face value; metres spent off-axis — diagonals, verticals, and the arcs themselves — cost 1.6×. So a long shallow diagonal or a tent over an obstacle pays a premium on its whole length, while a route that steps aside briefly and then runs parallel only pays on the short step. The constant is OFF_AXIS_COST at the top of the routing block if you want it stronger.

A side effect worth knowing: the router now prefers a 45° step over a gentle 22.5° drift for lateral moves, because the shallower angle spends more length off-axis — which matches how you'd actually detail a ductbank.
