Plan view in mm, +X east / +Y north, chambers drawn as internal box plus hatched wall annulus. Three demo chambers loaded so you can try it immediately.

The part that matters for the next step is the face model. Each chamber exposes four faces named in its local frame (N = +Y, E = +X, S = −Y, W = −X, rotating with the chamber), and faceGeom(chamber, face) returns the internal face in world coordinates:

{ p1, p2, mid, n, width, outerMid, bearing }

mid is the internal face midpoint, n the outward unit normal, outerMid the same point pushed through the wall to the external face, and bearing the compass bearing of the normal. Hover any face and you get that readout live.

There's a placeholder connection already wired in — "Connect faces" mode, pick an internal face on one chamber then another, and it draws a straight centreline between the two midpoints with the internal-face-to-internal-face length labelled. Verified against the demo: MH01·E to MH02·W at 9000 centres gives 7650. That block in draw() and pickFace() is what you'd swap out when the real logic goes in (offset from midpoint, angle constraints, invert levels).

Export gives you JSON keyed by chamber ref rather than internal ids, so it round-trips and is easy to feed elsewhere.
