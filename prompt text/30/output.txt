Version 30: examples, elevations and a 3D window of their own.

The example drawings have moved out of the ribbon into an Examples window of their own, in the Manage group: the AMS01 site of 105 manholes, the DCBuild test model with its three, and the demo drawing the tool opens with, each with a description and a Place button. Placing asks before replacing a drawing that has anything in it, and Undo brings the previous drawing back.

Right-click a face and its ring now offers Elevation, which opens a window showing that face: its runs listed with their level controls, and the to-scale section through the wall with the boundary box the conduits must keep to drawn dotted, the family's conduit window where the chamber came from Revit with one, the clear width of the face otherwise. A face with no runs still shows its box. Click a conduit in the section, or its row, and that run is selected on the plan and in the Runs window. Asking for another face, on the same manhole or another, replaces the view in the same window, and the side buttons in the Chambers window open it too; the old pop-up is gone.

The 3D view no longer takes over the plan. It opens in a window of its own, from the ribbon or key 3, sized as you like by the grip in its corner and redrawn to fit as it stretches. Drag to orbit, shift or right drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a chamber, an obstacle or a conduit run to select it, and right-click or hold for the ring, which offers Fit, Runs and Close 3D. Every buffer zone is drawn as a dotted box around its chamber or obstacle, as on the plan. The plan stays live beside it, and both follow every change and every selection.

Verified in a driven browser with a mouse and with emulated touch: the three examples placed from the window, the elevation opened from the ring and from a side button, its two conduits and dotted box, a conduit and a row selecting their run, the level buttons, the view replaced for another face, a face with no runs, the 3D window opened by key and by button with the plan still drawn beneath, its boxes and dotted buffer boxes, orbit, pan, zoom and pinch inside it, chamber and run picked by click and by tap, the ring inside the window and Close 3D from it, the grip stretching the window by mouse and by finger with the view redrawn to the new size, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 29, all passing with no console errors.
