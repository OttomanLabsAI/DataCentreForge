Version 37: the array is a picture, edited by clicking.

The typed rows are gone. A run's Array & level cluster now shows the section of the run as a picture — one circle per conduit in the spec's colour, rows stacked as they sit — seen along the run from its first chamber to its second, so left is left. A dotted slot at the open end of each row adds a conduit to that row, the dotted slot beneath the rows adds a new row, and a click on the last conduit of a row takes it away, the row going with its last conduit; a single conduit cannot be removed. Under the picture the reading says what it shows — 3 + 2, 5 conduits, 3 wide × 2 high, and which side the shorter rows sit on — and the choice of side, auto or left or right, appears only when the rows differ, since it means nothing otherwise. The picture redraws to the side chosen, the shorter row against it and the spare column on the open side, and the elevation list reads the array the same way. The screenshot you sent could not be kept with the archive, as pasted images cannot be saved.

Verified in a driven browser: a 3 over 2 run drawn as three conduits above two against the left, with dotted slots at the end of each row and one beneath, and no typed fields; the reading and the cluster summary saying 3 + 2, 5 conduits, 3 wide × 2 high, with auto naming left; a click on the second row's slot making it 3 + 3 and the side box going away for equal rows; the slot beneath adding a row of one; taking that lone conduit away removing the row; taking the top row's last conduit giving 2 over 3; packed right putting the short row against the right with the spare column on the left; a single conduit reading 1 wide and refusing to be taken away; the elevation list reading the array the same way; and the MV model battery working the picture instead of the fields, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 36, all passing with no console errors.
