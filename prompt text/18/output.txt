Version 18 — obstacles in three dimensions.

Every obstacle now has a top and a bottom, and three rules read straight from the Revit instance parameters (obstacle_around, obstacle_over, obstacle_under — the family's "obstable_" spelling is accepted too, and anything missing counts as allowed): whether a run may go around it, over it, or under it. The same three switches sit in the obstacle's own panel for anything drawn by hand, alongside its top and bottom levels.

The rules work exactly as asked. Around comes first: the plan is routed round every obstacle that allows it, and a run only ever crosses an around-obstacle when nothing gets round — and then it crosses as few of them as it can. Where a crossing is allowed, under is the default and over the fallback, and a run that already clears an obstacle in section is left straight. An obstacle allowing nothing at all is impassable and the run says so.

Once the plan is settled, the long section is solved with the same engine in the chainage–Z plane — same fittings, same minimum straights, same clearances — stepping under or over each crossed obstacle and keeping its vertical bends off the plan bends. A bank with lanes on deeper levels lifts its over-crossings by the depth of its lowest row. Chambers gain a Level 0 Z (the top row's centreline; taken from the conduit window top on a Revit import), so runs between chambers at different depths change level with real fittings too.

Each run now shows its long section in its panel: the crossed obstacles as boxes, the conduit at its own radius, the depths at both chambers and at the low point, and the laid length — the plan labels and the run list quote that laid length, and the plan marks each crossing where the run enters it ("▼ under OBS01"). The export carries the section vertices, bends, crossings and laid length; the Revit exporter now writes every flagged obstacle as its own box in family coordinates with its placement and rules, and the importer places them.

Verified in a driven browser: around preferred, under by default, over as fallback, lifted crossings for deeper lanes, honest refusal when only around is allowed and nothing gets round, a deep obstacle passed without bending, level changes between chambers, the panels and long section, export and import round trips, and a Revit import carrying rotated obstacles — plus the batteries from versions 14 to 17, all passing with no console errors.
