Version 44: a static model becomes a set of obstacles.

You asked for this two versions ago and I gave you keep-outs instead of obstacles, which is why the run in your screenshot said it was blocked while its Obstacles list said there were none. Now every element of a static model is a real obstacle for the runs of a dynamic one. Each of its manholes is a box between its lid and its base; each of its conduit banks is its encasement carried along its route, as wide as the box and as deep as the run's own depths. They appear in every dynamic run's Obstacles panel beside the drawing's own obstacles, named for what they are — S01 · static manhole, MVF-01·C → MVF-02·C · static conduits — each with the same choice of around, over or under for that run alone.

Around is the default and behaves as before: the run keeps clear of the box, whatever the drawing's avoidance switches say. Told to cross, the run goes straight in plan and steps in section, diving under the bank's box or riding over it at its true depth, and the drawing labels the crossing as it does for any obstacle. Over still respects the ground cover, so a shallow bank refuses with a reason rather than breaking the surface. A run that cannot pass now says exactly what is in its way, naming every manhole and bank, instead of the blunt "blocked — no way past the keep-outs". The choice is saved with the drawing and matched back to the same element when the file is opened. A static bank is no longer double-counted: its clearance is the one enforced while routing, so a run crossing it is not also flagged for being too close.

Verified in a driven browser: a static model offering its two manholes and its encased bank as obstacles, the bank 700 deep and 800 half-wide; a dynamic run across their path blocked while everything said around; its panel listing all three with around, over and under; told under, the run diving below the bank's box, reporting the crossing and its depth with four vertical bends; told over on a deeper bank, riding above it and staying under the cover; back to around, blocked again as before; a static manhole gone round with four bends or dived under at its base with none; the export naming the manhole and its choice and an import matching it back; and on the MV site all 152 static elements listed, with a run asked to dive under six of them at once refusing by name and saying there is no room to climb back, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 43, all passing with no console errors.
