Version 49: the opening drawing goes round its obstacle, and shows you it can be moved.

The drawing the tool opens with now sets its own obstacle to around, so the first thing on the screen is the two runs skirting it with a flight of bends — the routing at work. Everything else keeps the default from the last version: a new obstacle, and the manholes and banks of a static model, still pass runs underneath unless told otherwise. The Examples window's demo drawing is the same one, so placing it again gives the same picture.

Beside the box a ghost of it nudges sideways, with a pointer riding on its edge and a line above saying drag the obstacle — the runs find their way round. It sits over the box wherever the view is panned or zoomed, cannot be clicked, and holds still for anyone whose device asks for less motion. The first drag of a chamber or obstacle takes it away for good, as does a drawing that replaces this one or half a minute of leaving it alone; a fresh page brings it back.
