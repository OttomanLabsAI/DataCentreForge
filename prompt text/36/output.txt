Version 36: the MV model arrives from its IFC with its conduits, and runs are laid out row by row.

The LV site in the Examples window now has an MV sub-model, read from the IFC you sent: its 23 fibre manholes, 44 vaults and 8 pull boxes at their true positions, rotations, sizes and depths, in the same site coordinates as the LV model, so the two place together. The IFC carries no marks, so they are named MVF-01 to MVF-23, LVV-01 to LVV-44 and DPB-01 to DPB-08, each keeping its IFC element id so a later export refreshes it in place. Under the MV entry sits a checkbox, ticked by default: Recreate the conduits from the IFC as runs. With it on, adding the MV model or placing the whole site also recreates the 77 conduit banks that join two of its chambers, each as one run between the faces the conduits actually enter, with its rows as the model has them — 22 banks of 8, 8 and 8 on Ø128 duct, 45 of 2 over 2 or 4 abreast on Ø114, and the single Ø51 fibre conduits to the pull boxes — at the depth and pitch the model laid them, on specs made for those sizes when the drawing has none. Adding the model again refreshes the banks rather than doubling them, the status line says how many were recreated, and on the crowded site a handful of banks say why they cannot be routed, as any run does. The 442 conduit runs that leave the model with one end in the open cannot be runs and are left out. The reader that does all this is kept with the repository, so the example can be rebuilt from a later IFC.

Arrays are now given row by row. A run's Array & level cluster has a Per row field — 3 2 for three over two, 8 8 8 for the big banks — and every row sits on the same columns at the face's pitch, so the conduits line up vertically. A shorter row is packed to one side: auto packs it toward the side the next manhole lies on and, when that manhole is straight ahead, away from the face's other runs, and the option names the side it chose; left or right can be set instead, seen along the run from its first chamber to its second. The elevation, the 3D view and the route summary draw and count exactly those conduits, and the drawing's file carries the rows and the side, older files reading as equal rows.

Verified in a driven browser: a 3 + 2 run counting three wide, two high and five conduits; auto packing left with the next manhole to the left, right with it to the right, and away from a neighbouring run when it is straight ahead; the elevation drawing five conduits with the lower two under two of the upper three on the packed side; the 3D view giving one centreline per conduit; the panel reading 3 2 and taking 4 2 1 and a chosen side; the export carrying the rows and the side, and an older rows-by-columns file reading as equal rows; the MV model adding 75 chambers and 77 banks with their rows and new Ø128, Ø114 and Ø51 specs in under three seconds, refreshing without doubling, an MV face's elevation showing its 24 conduits, and the site placing 180 chambers with no runs when the box is unticked and the 77 banks when it is, plus the batteries from versions 14 to 35, all passing with no console errors.
